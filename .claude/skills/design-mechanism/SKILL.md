# Yoni's design mechanism

1. **Read the master first:** `~/AETHER/principles/DESIGN.md` (bible repo github.com/M-turtle/bible). In a cloud chat that can't see the Mac, read the copy at `docs/design/MECHANISM.md` in the repo, if one exists. Otherwise follow the summary below and say that the master could not be read.
2. **The Bible wins** (E1 options never capped, mock-up first, picture against picture, F1 cost first). Other design skills (impeccable's 3-card deal, its image generation) yield to it. Midjourney prompts, not paid image generation, for mood.
3. **Reference implementation:** TML-APP `docs/design/keycard/`. Copy its generator pattern (`source/round2/kc.py` shared parts + `source/keycard/kcf*.py` sections), its checker (`source/keycard/batch.py`) and its handoff folder shape (README, HANDOFF, DECISIONS, brand-book, screens, source).

## The mechanism in one screen
- Spread of 8–12 directions on the same screens and data (phone 390 + desk 1440, light + night) → his picks → round two → one pick → one object A to Z.
- Mock-ups are generated by code onto a Design canvas, every screen and every state, plus an Inventory board mapping today's pages.
- Render and check every board (overflow, overlap, hero size uniform, clipping, nothing under the tab bar). Then look at the sheets yourself before he sees anything.
- His words go into DECISIONS.md the same turn. Questions become Desk cards (`~/AETHER/principles/DESK.md`). Rules go into the shared code.
- Before building: the product's plan (the Stone for Aether/TML) names the design and **whose product it is**. TML's own portals and Aether's apps are different products.
- Handoff is a folder in the product's repo, merged into the working branch, with a generator that rebuilds every board byte for byte. Hand it over with a line Yoni pastes into the build chat. A cross-session message alone can sit unread when the permission modes differ.
