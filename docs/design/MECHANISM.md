**The loop for a new brand:** brief → spread of 8–10 directions (Midjourney prompts he runs + Design canvases, same 3 screens, phone+desktop)
→ his picks (no cap on the spread) → round two → pick → book written into the brand's Design System → export → full mock-up (every screen, state, width, look) → approval → build, picture against picture.

## From pick to build: how a mock-up is made, checked and handed over (proven on the TML Key card, 2026-10-19)

Reference implementation, copy it, don't reinvent: TML-APP `docs/design/keycard/` (`source/` = generator, `README.md` = the handoff shape).

1. **Spread, then one object.** The brief as a problem (what the picture must answer, for whom, at what counts), never a subject → the map (axes × values: the object, the first question it answers, viewpoint and composition, the medium as executed, the motion law; one cell per option; no two cells share more than one value; the brand's fixed layer in every cell) → a study per cell, looked at and linked (two or three real works opened; three lines: what it does with line, space and hierarchy; what we take; what we refuse); his Midjourney frames, when he sends them, are the cells' references → one fresh context per cell, never shown the other cells or any earlier round (a fan-out: on his word, cost stated, Bible F1) → the gates (step 3: the fingerprint; the stranger's test) → 20–30 small studies in one state, with the map, so he sees coverage and not only pictures → his picks → the picks only, full: phone 390 + desk 1440, day + night, motion, every state → his one pick → one object A to Z (the idea that carries the data, not tokens on a template). The map persists per subject (`design/<subject>/MAP.md`: cells tried, his words, dead cells); a new round opens new cells, and a round that redraws a covered cell is refused. Rules of the kind "change only what he named" belong to a locked mock-up, never to a spread. (Yoni 2026-11-06, after ten Iris rounds came out as one drawing: "they pretty much all look the same ... not using different design methods, vibes"; the diagnosis: https://claude.ai/artifact/T8X3XV8staqCz3rvpyhU8L.)
2. **Mock-ups are generated by code, never hand-drawn.** One Python generator per product: shared parts (tokens, the hero object, shells: phone, desk, sheet, dialog) + one file per section writing Design-canvas boards (`*.dc.html` + `canvas.json`). Every size, colour, word and keyframe lives in the generator, so the builder copies values, not pixels. A rule Yoni gives ("the card is one size on every page") is enforced in the shared part, where no call site can break it.
3. **Every board is rendered and checked before he sees it.** Playwright renders phone 390×844, desk 1440 and desk at 390, and prints: overflow (CHECK), text overlap (OVERLAP), the hero object's size and position on every board (must be uniform), clipping inside it (CLIP), content under the tab bar (MAIN-OVER). Anything non-zero is fixed before publishing. Then look at contact sheets yourself. "By the numbers" is not looked at. For a spread, two more gates before he sees anything: a **fingerprint** per option (composition boxes, element counts, stroke widths, palette, fonts, filters, motion type); two options too close are one direction and the duplicate is redrawn; and the **stranger's test**: a fresh context looks at the captionless sheet and answers how many artists made it, what each option means, and which obey the fixed layer; the sheet ships only when the count is at least the number of options minus one and every option is in the language. A medium is executed, never named: the shared generator holds a technique library (paper grain, ink bleed, pencil tooth, hatching, misregistration, cyanotype, cut paper, thread) with a proof board per medium, and an option whose medium reads as flat vector fails.
4. **The full mock-up covers every screen and every state**, plus an Inventory board mapping every page of today's code to its new board (B3; nothing unmapped). Anything left out is listed with the reason (e.g. a broken-window feature).
5. **Motion is part of the spec**: named keyframes in the generator, rest frame pixel-identical to the board (checked), reduced-motion respected, loop boards for the key moment.
6. **Every answer is written down the same turn**: his words verbatim in DECISIONS.md, the Desk card closed, rules turned into code in the shared part.
7. **Handoff = a folder in the product's repo, merged into the working branch**, so any chat on any account can build without the design chat. The folder holds: `README.md` (start here: status, whose app, read order, rebuild commands), `HANDOFF.md` (gates, rules, states, motion, every screen), `DECISIONS.md` (his words, dated), `brand-book/` (opens without a claude.ai login), `screens/` (WebP of every board, phone + desk + desk at 390), and `source/` (the generator, proven to rebuild every board byte for byte from the repo). Repo is the master; claude.ai pages are copies.
8. **The Stone (or the product's plan) names the design before anyone builds**, including whose product it is (e.g. TML's own portals vs Aether's apps for every company; Yoni 2026-10-19: "There's ether and there's TML, and they're not the same").
9. **Hand over by a line Yoni pastes into the build chat**, never by a cross-session message alone: chats in a different permission mode hold such messages unread.
